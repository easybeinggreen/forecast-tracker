The Electoral district of Evelyn and Mornington was an electoral district of the Victorian Legislative Assembly. 38°00′S 145°20′E﻿ / ﻿38.000°S 145.333°E﻿ / -38.000; 145.333

The district was based on the counties of Evelyn and Mornington, and defined in the Victoria Constitution Act 1855.[1]
