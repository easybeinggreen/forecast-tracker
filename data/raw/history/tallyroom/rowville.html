Rowville – Victoria 2026

LIB 3.7%

Incumbent MP
Kim Wells, since 2014. Previously member for Wantirna 1992-2002, member for Scoresby 2002-2014.

  • Geography
  • History

Geography
South-eastern Melbourne. Rowville covers the suburbs of Knoxfield, Lysterfield, Lysterfield South, Rowville, Rowville East, Scoresby and Wantirna South. Rowville covers the southern half of the City of Knox.

History
Rowville was created in 2014 as a successor to Scoresby.

The district of Scoresby was most recently created at the 2002 election.  It had previously existed from 1945 to 1976, when it was always held by the Liberal Party.

Scoresby was won in 2002 by Liberal MP Kim Wells, whose seat of Wantirna had been abolished in the recent redistribution. Wells was re-elected in 2006 and 2010, and again as member for Rowville in 2014, 2018 and 2022.

Candidates
Sitting Liberal MP Kim Wells is not running for re-election.

  • Lars Hiemstra (Victorian Socialists)
  • Dave Massey (Greens)
  • Scott Robson (Libertarian)
  • Muhammad Shahbaz (Labor)
  • Bradley Watt (One Nation)
  • Max Williams (Liberal)

Assessment
The Liberal Party will probably retain this seat.

Booth breakdown

Booths have been divided into three areas: east, north-west and south-west.

The Liberal Party won a majority of the two-party-preferred vote in two out of three areas, with 51.1% in the south-west and 53.7% in the east. Labor narrowly won in the north-west. The Liberal Party won more comfortably on the pre-poll and other votes.

Independent candidate Nicole Seymour came third, with a primary vote ranging from 12.4% in the north-west to 15.4% in the east.
